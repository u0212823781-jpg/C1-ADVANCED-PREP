Speaking · Part 1

Interview

The examiner will ask about your life, interests, studies, experiences and future plans. Respond naturally, express your views clearly and develop each answer without speaking for too long.

01

Useful Language

Express Yourself Naturally

I'd probably say that…
What appeals to me most is…
I tend to…
One thing I've come to appreciate is…
If I had to choose, I'd…
I'm particularly keen on…
What I find most rewarding is…

02

Go Beyond a Short Answer

Basic

I enjoy living in Madrid.

Better

I enjoy living in Madrid because there's always something to do.

Developed C1 Answer

What appeals to me most about living in Madrid is the variety. There are plenty of cultural activities and green spaces, so it's easy to find something that suits your mood.

03

Make It Personal

Add a Personal Perspective

Give a genuine reason.
Include one relevant detail.
Refer to a personal experience.
Explain how something affects you.
Avoid memorised answers.
Keep your response focused.
Small, specific details make your answers sound authentic.
04

Dealing With Unexpected Questions

Give Yourself a Moment

That's an interesting question.
I haven't really considered that before, but…
My first reaction would be to say…
If I had to choose, I'd probably…
It depends to some extent on…
Pause briefly to organise your ideas, but do not overuse delaying phrases.

See It in Action · Model Answer
What do you enjoy most about learning English?

What I enjoy most is being able to communicate with people from different countries. It also gives me access to films, books and online content in the original language. Recently, I watched a series without subtitles and realised how much my listening had improved, which was really motivating.
