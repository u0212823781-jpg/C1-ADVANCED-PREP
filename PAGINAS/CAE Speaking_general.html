03

The four Speaking parts

Part 1 —

Interview

  • Personal information
  • Interests, studies and career
  • Developing opinions
About 2 minutes
Part 2 —

Long turn

  • 3 photographs — choose 2
  • Compare and evaluate
  • Speak for 1 minute
  • Partner response: 30 seconds
Part 3 —

Collaborative task

  • Discuss written prompts
  • Exchange and evaluate ideas
  • Develop your partner's contributions
2-min discussion + 1-min decision
Part 4 —

Discussion

  • Linked to the Part 3 topic
  • Explore abstract issues
  • Justify and qualify opinions
About 5 minutes
04

Smart exam tips

Part 1
  • Develop answers beyond personal facts
  • Support views with relevant detail
  • Use precise and varied language
  • Sound natural, not rehearsed
Part 2
  • Compare rather than describe
  • Address both questions
  • Speculate, evaluate and justify
  • Organise the full minute clearly
Part 3
  • Engage actively with your partner
  • Develop and respond to ideas
  • Evaluate rather than list
  • Negotiate a clear decision
Part 4
  • Discuss ideas in greater depth
  • Consider different perspectives
  • Support arguments with examples
  • React naturally to your partner
05

What are the examiners looking for?

Grammatical Resource

Use varied simple and complex forms with control.

Lexical Resource

Use precise vocabulary to develop complex ideas.

Discourse Management

Produce coherent contributions that are easy to follow.

Pronunciation

Use clear pronunciation, stress and intonation.

Interactive Communication

Initiate, respond and develop the interaction.

Global Achievement

Communicate effectively on familiar, unfamiliar and abstract topics.
