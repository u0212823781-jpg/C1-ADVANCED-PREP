Exam Essentials

Everything you need to know about your C1 Advanced exam.

01

Exam structure

The exam assesses five skills across four papers and takes approximately 4 hours.

Use of English

  • Parts 1–4
  • 28 marks
  • Within the 90-minute paper
TARGET · 16/28

Reading

  • Parts 5–8
  • 50 marks
  • Within the 90-minute paper
TARGET · 32/50

Writing

  • 2 parts
  • 2 tasks
  • 1h 30 min
TARGET · 24/40

Listening

  • 4 parts
  • 30 questions
  • About 40 min
TARGET · 18/30

Speaking

  • 4 parts
  • 15 min per pair
  • 23 min for three candidates
TARGET · 45/75

Practice guide: these raw-score targets are approximate. Your official result is based on your Cambridge English Scale score, with 180 as the C1 threshold.

02

Scoring & Grades

Grade A 200–210 C2
Grade B 193–199 C1
Grade C 180–192 C1
Level B2 160–179 B2
C1 pass threshold: 180
03

Exam Day

  • Arrive on time
  • Bring valid photo ID
  • Bring pens, pencil, eraser & sharpener
  • No electronic devices or watches
  • Follow your centre's instructions
  • Manage your time carefully
04

Results & Certificates

Keep your Confirmation of Entry to access your results online.

Digital exam certificates are sent to centres 3–4 weeks after the exam.

Paper-based exam certificates are sent to centres 7–10 weeks after the exam.
